Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

1. Who we are

Dois Bolsos (“the app”) is published by T0LD, an individual developer based in Brazil. Contact: t0ld.dev@gmail.com.

2. Summary

• There is no account, login or sign-up.

• Your transactions, accounts, budgets and settings stay only on your device.

• We have no server of our own: we, the developers, receive none of your data.

• There are no ads, no usage analytics and no tracking.

• The Premium purchase is processed by Google Play.

• Backups and reports leave the device only when you export them yourself and choose where to send them.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores locally, in a database on the device itself: your profiles (personal/business), accounts and cards, categories, transactions (amounts, dates, descriptions, installments and recurring bills), receipts (photos and PDFs) you attach, budgets, savings goals and their deposits, settings (theme, reminders, MEI mode) and a minimal technical record that Premium was purchased, so it works offline. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else, is not sold and is not shared.

It stays on the device until you delete it (for example by clearing the app data in Android settings) or uninstall the app.

4. What the app does not do

The app shows no ads, uses no analytics or advertising SDKs, builds no behavioral profile and does not ask for access to your contacts, location, microphone or bank data. The camera is used only if you yourself choose to photograph a receipt. It does not connect to banks or read SMS messages.

5. Premium purchase (Google Play)

Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google: the app never sees or stores your card number or other payment details.

To know whether you own Premium (including when you restore the purchase), the app queries Google Play on your device and receives technical purchase information such as the product identifier, purchase state and a token. We keep only a minimal record of this on the device so the app works offline. Google handles its data under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

6. Backup, export and sharing

When you tap “Export backup”, the app creates a file with your data and opens Android’s share menu; where the file goes (Drive, email, another app) is your choice, and from then on it is governed by that destination’s rules. The same applies to PDF or CSV reports (a Premium feature). We do not receive these files.

When you restore a backup, the app reads the file you chose, on this device only. Keep your backups somewhere safe: they contain your financial information.

Statement import (Premium): the app reads only the OFX or CSV file you choose, on this device only; the content is not sent to anyone. The category rules the app learns from your corrections stay on the device and are included in your backup.

7. Notifications (reminders)

If you turn on bill reminders, the app schedules local notifications on the device itself, with no server and no internet. You can turn them off at any time in Settings or in Android’s notification settings.

8. Widget

The home-screen balance widget shows the month summary using a small data file kept on the device itself. Nothing is sent out.

9. Permissions

• Notifications: only for bill reminders, if you turn them on.

• Internet and billing (Google Play): used only to query and complete the Premium purchase.

• Camera (optional): only when you tap “Take photo” to attach a receipt. The photo stays on the device.

• Receipts: photos and PDFs you attach to a transaction are copied to the app’s private folder, stay only on the device and are deleted together with the transaction. They only leave the device if you export a backup (.zip file) or share the receipt.

• Biometrics/screen lock (optional): used only to unlock the app; the Android system does the verification.

• File access: the app only reads a file when you pick a backup to restore, and only writes/shares what you export.

10. Android automatic backup

Android and Google may include app data in the device’s automatic backup to your Google account, depending on your system settings. This is done by the system, not by the app or by us; you can turn it off in Android settings.

11. Your rights

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side: you already have full control on the device. Depending on where you live (for example Brazil’s LGPD, the EU/UK GDPR or similar laws) you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection; for any question or request, write to t0ld.dev@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

12. Children

The app is a personal-finance tool for adults and is not directed at children. We do not collect data from anyone, including children.

13. Security

Data stays in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s app isolation. No method is completely secure: keep a screen lock on your device and your backups in a protected place.

App lock (optional): if you turn on “Lock with fingerprint/face”, the app asks Android to confirm who you are (fingerprint, face or the device PIN/pattern). The system does this: the app only receives “confirmed” or “not confirmed” and never accesses, reads or stores biometric data. The “Hide in recent apps” option only asks Android to hide the app’s screen in the recent-apps switcher and in screenshots.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will arrive with an app update and on the public page, with an updated effective date.

15. Contact

T0LD, Brazil. Email: t0ld.dev@gmail.com.
